Political Intelligence & Campaign 
Operating System (PICOS)
Version: 1.0  |  Status: Planning  |  Prepared By: Avhad Enterprises  |  Pilot Client: Uday Sangle  |  Location: Sinnar, 
Nashik, Maharashtra
SUMMARY
PICOS is a Political Intelligence & Campaign Operating System designed to help political leaders, campaign teams, 
volunteers, and war rooms manage every aspect of an election campaign and constituency operations through a 
centralized platform.
BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
Primary Objectives: Increase campaign efficiency; Centralize campaign data; Improve decision-making; Track voter 
sentiment; Manage volunteers; Monitor opponents; Run digital campaigns; Provide real-time intelligence; Support 
election-winning strategies.
USER TYPES
Super Admin: Avhad Enterprises (Full Access). Candidate: Uday Sangle (View everything, Approve campaigns, 
Approve communications, View analytics). Campaign Manager: Manage campaign operations, Assign tasks, Manage 
volunteers, Track performance. Intelligence Team: Research, Competitor tracking, Issue tracking, Sentiment analysis, 
Media analysis. Content Team: Social media, Design, Video, Website content, Speech content. V olunteer: Limited 
access, Assigned tasks, Booth reporting, Village reporting. Booth Coordinator: Manage booth-level operations, Track 
local workers, Collect local intelligence. Constituency Coordinator: Manage multiple booths, Track local issues, 
Coordinate events.
CORE MODULES
Module 01 Political Intelligence; Module 02 Constituency Intelligence; Module 03 Booth Intelligence; Module 04 
V oter Intelligence; Module 05 Issue Mapping; Module 06 V olunteer Management; Module 07 WhatsApp Operations; 
Module 08 Media Monitoring; Module 09 Social Media Management; Module 10 Event Management; Module 11 War 
Room Dashboard; Module 12 AI Assistant; Module 13 Election Analytics; Module 14 Grievance Management; 
Module 15 Knowledge Base.
MODULE 01 - POLITICAL INTELLIGENCE
Purpose: Track political environment. Features: Candidate Profile, Opponent Profile, Party Intelligence, Political 
Timeline, Political Events, Relationship Mapping, Influencer Mapping, Political Risk Tracking, Political Opportunity 
Tracking. Outputs: Daily Political Intelligence Report, Weekly Political Intelligence Report, Monthly Intelligence 
Report.
MODULE 02 - CONSTITUENCY INTELLIGENCE
Store: Constituency, Villages, Wards, Zones, Population, Communities, Demographics, Economics, Historical V oting 
Trends. Outputs: Constituency Health Score, Political Opportunity Score, Community Influence Map.
