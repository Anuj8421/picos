# Project Context

Last organized: 2026-06-10 Asia/Calcutta.

This project is the working context base for a Political Intelligence and Campaign Operating System centered on Uday Sangle and the Sinnar Assembly constituency in Nashik district, Maharashtra.

## Master Objective

Build a complete political intelligence, campaign operations, voter analysis, digital influence, and war-room system for Sinnar.

The master strategic question for all work is:

> Will this increase Uday Sangle's political strength, voter trust, organizational power, public support, or electoral performance in Sinnar?

If a task does not support that question, it is not a priority.

## Project Owner And Product Frame

Source documents identify Anuj Avhad / Avhad Enterprises as the project owner or system builder. The product concept is PICOS: Political Intelligence and Campaign Operating System.

PICOS is intended to centralize campaign data, improve decisions, track voter sentiment, manage volunteers, monitor opponents, run digital operations, and support election-readiness work.

Primary pilot client: Uday Sangle.

## Primary Subject

Uday Sangle is the central subject of this project.

Known from the provided documents:

- Location: Sinnar, Nashik district, Maharashtra.
- Public identity: political leader, entrepreneur, social activist, sports activist.
- Founder of Sahyadri Yuva Manch.
- Political history: long association with Shiv Sena, joined NCP Sharad Pawar faction before the 2024 Assembly election, contested Sinnar in 2024, later expelled from NCP(SP), and reportedly moved toward or into BJP-aligned circles.
- Family: wife Sheetal Sangle, former President of Nashik Zilla Parishad, formerly associated with Shiv Sena and reported to have supported the Eknath Shinde faction during the Shiv Sena split.

Working perception from the documents:

- Strong grassroots leader.
- Strong worker loyalty.
- Rural and village-level reach.
- Youth support.
- Major challenger to Manikrao Kokate.
- Not yet an Assembly winner.
- Has political-transition perception risk due to party movement.

## Sinnar Electoral Context

The documents frame Sinnar as a fluid constituency influenced by local leadership, Maratha and OBC groups, agricultural issues, cooperative networks, youth employment, roads, water, irrigation, crop prices, and anti-incumbency.

Working 2024 result value:

- Manikrao Kokate: 138,565 votes.
- Uday Sangale/Sangle: 97,681 votes.
- Margin: 40,884 votes.

Important conflict:

- Some project documents list Uday Sangle at approximately 105,137 votes and Kokate around 145,501 votes.
- Treat the 97,681 / 138,565 figures as the current working value because they are repeated in the competitor-analysis sources and public result tables, but obtain official ECI Form 20 before using the figures publicly or analytically.

## Competitor Landscape

### Manikrao Kokate

Role in documents: principal competitor.

Known profile:

- Multiple-term MLA from Sinnar.
- NCP Ajit Pawar faction.
- Former Maharashtra cabinet minister.
- Strong grassroots, cooperative, administrative, and patronage networks.
- Major vote base in 2024.

Vulnerabilities:

- Anti-incumbency after multiple terms.
- Frequent party switching.
- Legal and reputation risk around the EWS housing-related case.
- Limited digital-first positioning compared with the opportunity available to Sangle.

External update:

- Some PDFs state that Bombay High Court suspended sentence but did not stay conviction. A later Times of India report dated 2025-12-22 says the Supreme Court stayed the conviction only to the extent that it would not cause MLA disqualification. Verify before public use.

### Rajabhau Waje

Role in documents: secondary or potential major competitor.

Known profile:

- Former Sinnar MLA.
- Nashik MP elected in 2024, according to provided documents.
- Farmer-centric image.
- Political family background.
- Associated in the documents with Shiv Sena factional politics.
- His panel reportedly took control of Sinnar APMC from Kokate supporters in 2023.

Strategic implication:

- If he contests locally, he can split or reshape the anti-Kokate vote.
- If he remains focused on parliamentary duties, Sangle can position as the local, continuously present alternative.

### Other Competitors

Potential candidates include Shiv Sena factional candidates, independents, sarpanches, cooperative leaders, and smaller local formations. Even low-probability candidates can affect margins village by village.
